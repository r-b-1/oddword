## Design Context

### Users

Casual trivia players taking quick breaks, on phones and desktops. They want a satisfying daily challenge and to discover the answers they missed.

### Brand Personality

Playful, curious, punchy. The user approved the Oddword name and the proposed puzzle-club direction.

### Aesthetic Direction

Ruled notebook paper, pencil doodles, crumpled and folded paper scenery, ink typography, and orange accents. The user wants the satisfying score-driven travel of Krillion with a playful, handmade Scribblenauts-like feel. An original paper-plane character climbs through a scrolling notebook as points increase, discovering gardens, paper mountains, clouds, and stars. Keep the Oddword name and asterisk motif. Use Bricolage Grotesque for expressive display lettering and Manrope for readable controls.

### Design Principles

- Make the prompt and next action instantly clear.
- Reward curiosity: show all accepted answers after the game and highlight the best pick.
- Keep scoring honest about the curated answer bank.
- Use strong type and intentional space, with accessible contrast and reduced motion.
- Make mobile play as complete as desktop play.
- Make points feel like a journey: travel is proportional to score, milestones reveal new scenery, and saved games restore the same position.
